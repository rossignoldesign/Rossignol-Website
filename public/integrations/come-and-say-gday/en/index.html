From the mountains to the reef, Australia is for adventurers!

A land of contrasts, diverse landscapes and unbridled wilderness, Australia’s outdoor adventures are as big as your imagination.

Episode 1: Wild discovery

Vancouver photographer Matt Massa heads across the Outback, through the Daintree Rainforest, and into the Great Barrier Reef to connect deeper with nature and Australian culture.

Episode 2: Surfies and sunshine

Matt heads to Byron Bay to get a taste of Australia’s laid-back beach culture, food and friendly faces. He’ll take to the seas to kayak, surf and even spot a dolphin or two!

Episode 3: Finding Your Roots

Matt enjoys two incredible Indigenous experiences that offer deeper insights into Australia’s history and culture before wrapping up his trip to Australia.
