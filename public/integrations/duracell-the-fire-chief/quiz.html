Question 1/7

Who celebrated the first Halloween, and when?

Answer:

The Ancient Celtic people of Ireland, who celebrated the holiday of Samhain thousands of years ago, by wearing costumes and having bonfires to ward off spirits, which has evolved into many of the Halloween traditions we know today.
Question 2/7

What does the name Halloween mean?

Answer:

‘All Hallows Eve’ - As Halloween grew, so did a religious holiday called All Saints Day which was thought to have been placed on November 1st, out of reverence to the ancient tradition of celebrating Halloween in Celtic culture, the night before.
Question 3/7

When did pumpkin carving originate from?

Answer:

Ancient Ireland. Much like the origins of Halloween itself, pumpkin carving comes from Ancient Ireland, and the legend of Stingy Jack, also known as Jack o' the Lantern.
Tip from Canadian Association of Fire Chiefs (CAFC): Jack-o-lanterns may be a time-honoured tradition, but they also represent a serious fire hazard. Instead of using a candle inside the pumpkin, place a small flashlight or battery-powered candle loaded with a trusted battery, like Duracell Optimum. This will keep your home’s decorations shining bright night after night.
